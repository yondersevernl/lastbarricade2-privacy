Privacy policy

Effective 6 October 2026 · Android package: com.yobo.lastbarricade2; com.sonbarikat.two

This policy describes the Android game Last Barricade 2 (Son Barikat 2), published by Yobo / OnderGames.

Information the game handles

The current game works offline. It does not require an account, collect or transmit personal information to Yobo / OnderGames, or include advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising identifier.

Data stored on your device

Campaign progress, upgrades, language choice and sound settings are saved locally in the app's storage. This information is not sent to our servers. You can delete it using Android's app storage settings or by uninstalling the game. Removing this data also removes your saved progress.

Google Play and testing

Google Play handles installation, updates and test participation under Google's own privacy policy. Google may make app quality or test information available through Play Console. This is separate from the game's local save data. See Google's privacy policy.

Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve the issue, retain it only as needed for support or applicable obligations, and do not sell it. Avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. You may contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Children

The game contains zombie horror, blood and violence and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the game's data practices change and display the new effective date. For privacy questions or support, contact Yobo / OnderGames at yondersevernl@gmail.com.
