Privacy Policy

This policy covers Renkio: Color Puzzle (Renkio: Renk Çekimi), published by Yobo (package com.yobo.renkio) and the earlier OnderGames edition (package com.colorpull.game).

Data and offline play

Renkio works offline and requires no account. The game does not collect or send personal data to Yobo, OnderGames or third parties. It contains no advertising, analytics or in-app purchases.

Information on your device

Your language choice, sound setting, tutorial completion, level progress, best scores and unfinished game are stored only in the app on your device. The initial language follows your device language. No cloud synchronisation or developer backup is provided.

Deleting your progress

Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to delete these local records. Neither Yobo nor OnderGames holds a copy of them.

Platform services and children

Google Play and Android may process information under their own privacy policies when you install, update or use their services. This policy describes the game itself. Renkio does not request personal information from children or other players.

Contact and changes

If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. Contact: yondersevernl@gmail.com. Changes to the game's data practices will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
