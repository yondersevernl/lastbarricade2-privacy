Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Android package: com.yobo.hotelescape

This policy describes Hotel Escape, the Android game published by Yobo / OnderGames.

Information the game handles

The game works offline. It does not require an account, collect or transmit personal information to Yobo / OnderGames, or include advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising identifier. The game is free and has no in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

Floor progress, checkpoint inventories, upgrades, language choice and sound settings are stored locally in the app's storage. They are not sent to our servers. You can erase local data through Android's app storage settings or by uninstalling the game. Erasing it removes your saved progress and preferences.

Google Play

Google Play handles installation, updates and test participation under Google's privacy policy. Google may make app quality or test information available through Play Console. This is separate from the game's local save data.

Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use this information to respond and resolve your issue, keep it only as needed for support or applicable obligations, and do not sell it. Avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Children

The game contains zombie horror, blood and violence and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information through support.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date if the game's data practices change. For privacy questions, requests or support, contact Yobo / OnderGames at yondersevernl@gmail.com.
